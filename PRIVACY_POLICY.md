# Privacy Policy for Weather Pro

**Last updated:** December 2024

Weather Pro ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Weather Pro mobile application (the "Service").

This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service and the choices you have associated with that data.

## Information Collection and Use

### Location Data
- **Purpose**: We collect location data to provide current weather information for your area
- **Collection Method**: GPS coordinates when you grant location permission
- **Usage**: Location data is used only to fetch weather information and is not stored permanently
- **Third-party Sharing**: Location coordinates are shared with WeatherAPI.com to retrieve weather data

### Weather Data
- **Data Source**: Weather information is retrieved from WeatherAPI.com
- **Caching**: Weather data is temporarily cached on your device to improve app performance and reduce data usage
- **No Personal Information**: Weather data does not contain personally identifiable information

### Network Information
- **Purpose**: We access network state to determine internet connectivity
- **Usage**: Used to provide appropriate error messages when offline and manage data requests

## Data Storage and Security

### Local Storage
- Weather data is cached locally on your device for up to 30 minutes
- No personal information is stored permanently
- Cache data is automatically cleared when the app is uninstalled

### Data Security
- All network communications use HTTPS encryption
- No user accounts or personal profiles are created
- No data is transmitted to our servers

## Third-Party Services

This app uses the following third-party service:

### WeatherAPI.com
- **Purpose**: Provides weather data and forecasts
- **Data Shared**: Location coordinates (latitude/longitude) and search queries
- **Privacy Policy**: https://www.weatherapi.com/privacy.aspx

### Google Play Services
- **Purpose**: Location services
- **Data Shared**: Location coordinates for weather retrieval
- **Privacy Policy**: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Permissions

### Required Permissions
- **Internet Access**: Required to fetch weather data
- **Network State Access**: To check internet connectivity
- **Location Access**: To provide weather for your current location (optional)
- **Wake Lock**: To ensure data synchronization completes

### Optional Permissions
- Location permissions are optional - you can use the app by manually entering city names

## Children's Privacy

Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
