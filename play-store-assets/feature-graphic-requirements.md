# Play Store Assets Requirements

## Required Assets for Google Play Store

### 1. App Icon
- **Format**: PNG (no transparency)
- **Size**: 512 x 512 pixels
- **Requirements**:
  - High-resolution icon for Play Store listing
  - Should match your app's adaptive icon design

### 2. Feature Graphic
- **Format**: JPG or PNG (no transparency)
- **Size**: 1024 x 500 pixels
- **Requirements**:
  - Used at the top of your store listing
  - Should showcase your app's key features
  - Text should be readable on mobile devices

### 3. Screenshots (Required)
- **Phone Screenshots**:
  - Minimum 2, maximum 8 screenshots
  - Minimum dimension: 320px
  - Maximum dimension: 3840px
  - Aspect ratio: Between 16:9 and 9:16

- **7-inch Tablet Screenshots** (Optional but recommended):
  - Same requirements as phone screenshots

- **10-inch Tablet Screenshots** (Optional):
  - Same requirements as phone screenshots

### 4. Store Listing Information
- **App Title**: Maximum 50 characters
- **Short Description**: Maximum 80 characters
- **Full Description**: Maximum 4000 characters
- **App Category**: Weather
- **Content Rating**: Everyone
- **Privacy Policy URL**: Required for apps that request permissions
